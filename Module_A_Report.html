3. Windowed Audio Feature Extraction

Audio signals were downsampled to 22,050 Hz and segmented into 1-second frames. Features extracted per window:

  • RMS Energy: Quantifies sound pressure levels.
  • Pitch (f0_mean): Measured via fast YIN algorithm within C2–C7 ranges.
  • MFCCs: 13 spectral envelope coefficients.
